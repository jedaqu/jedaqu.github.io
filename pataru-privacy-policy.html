Privacy Policy — Pataru

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Pataru is an Android calculator application developed by JEDAQU.

1. Personal Data

Pataru does not collect, store, or transmit users' personal data.

2. Usage Data

Pataru does not collect information about calculations performed, expressions entered, results, usage habits, or other activity data.

3. Internet Connection and Servers

Pataru operates locally on the device and does not require an Internet connection to perform its calculator functions. It does not use its own servers to process calculations.

4. Advertising, Tracking, and Analytics

Pataru contains no advertisements, does not use tracking technologies for advertising purposes, and does not incorporate third-party analytics services to collect information about application usage.

5. Data Sharing

Because Pataru does not collect personal or usage data, it does not share or sell user information to third parties.

6. Security

The application is designed to perform its functions locally, without sending calculator input data to an external server.

7. Changes to This Policy

If Pataru's features change in a way that affects data processing, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.
